1. Executive Summary & Core Mission

Skill Bridge is an AI-powered conversational web application built for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH). Its mission is to empower underserved youth, informal sector workers, rural artisans, and low-literacy citizens across India to transition from low-income, vulnerable occupations into skilled, high-growth, government-certified career pathways.

Unlike traditional career portals that assume high literacy, CV-writing skills, or English proficiency, Skill Bridge operates on a Voice-First, Vernacular-First, and Cognitive-Bridge architecture. It listens to a citizen's voice in their mother tongue, understands their traditional family craft and current job, isolates their implicit transferable skills, bridges them into formal accredited certifications (aligned with NSDC, PMKVY 4.0, PM Vishwakarma, and Skill India), and unlocks direct financial benefits and government subsidies.

2. Target Demographics & Grassroots Problem Solved

The Grassroots Problem: Conventional skilling programs treat candidates as complete novices, ignoring years of practical, real-world experience (e.g., an electrician's assistant already knows AC circuits, tools, and site safety, but lacks formal DC solar photovoltaic knowledge). Furthermore, linguistic barriers and scheme fragmentation keep citizens from accessing billions of rupees in government grants, toolkits, and low-interest credit.
  • Cognitive Bridge Intelligence: Re-values generational or informal labor, demonstrating that citizens already possess 30%–50% of the skills required for their target role.
  • Multilingual Voice Onboarding: Complete voice-driven interaction in 13 Indian languages.
  • Automated Scheme Matching: Matches candidates with exact central schemes based on their trade, family income, and social category (OBC, SC, ST, EWS, General).
  • Supervisor Admin Dashboard: Centralized digital dossier providing live batch tracking, serial ID lookup, and instant CSV reporting.

3. Core Architectural Philosophy

Principle Implementation Details
Voice-First & Multimodal Integrated Web Speech API (SpeechRecognition & SpeechSynthesis) with server-side proxy (/api/tts) fetching authentic native Indian language audio. Interactive visual fallback chips for silent environments.
Cognitive Bridging Explicit AI logic connecting prior experience (e.g., Construction Worker → Licensed Electrician, Driver → Logistics Fleet Coordinator, Farmer → Agri-Tech Drone Pilot).
Zero Authentication Friction Instant access with a 10-digit mobile number and automated 4-digit OTP verification simulation (4-8-2-9). No password fatigue or SMS delays during presentations.
Standardized Citizen Serial ID Unique state-district-serial identification format (e.g., TN-32-101) assigned to every citizen profile for instant institutional lookup.
Persistent Local State Client-side reactive store backed by browser localStorage (skill_bridge_state_v1 and skillbridge_admin_learners_v4). Zero database dependency required for hackathon demos.
Dual-Chassis Layout Renders as an iPhone-style centered frame on desktops and a native smartphone container on mobile devices, with an expandable wide institutional view for the Admin Portal.

7. Supervisor Admin Dashboard Features

  • Institutional Scale Metrics: Tracks 1,288+ candidates across aggregate batches with live metrics on active learners, certifications, and average completion rates.
  • Citizen Serial ID Lookup: Real-time lookup for candidate identifiers (e.g. TN-32-101) with an instant elevated Dossier Card.
  • Interactive Milestone Verification: Allows training officers to inspect and toggle individual roadmap step completions.
  • Official CSV Export: One-click download of a standard spreadsheet containing all 13 candidate parameters for state and central government reporting.
  • Simulated SMS Dispatch: Supervisors can send status updates and milestone nudges directly to the candidate's mobile number.

8. SIH Presentation Demo Ergonomics

Designed for Rapid Live Presentations: The top persistent DemoToolbar includes a 1-click "Quick Demo" button that instantly pre-fills the profile of Rohith Kumar (19-year-old Electrical Assistant from Tamil Nadu, OBC, aiming for Solar Technician). It generates the 5-stage roadmap, syncs to the Admin Portal, and loads the active UI in under 500ms.
